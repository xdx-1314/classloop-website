Privacy Policy

ClassLoop works without an account, advertising, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not operate a server that receives your schedules, assignments, grades, focus sessions, or attendance records.

Information stored by ClassLoop

Courses, assignments, countdowns, grades, attendance, preferences, focus sessions, and basic usage counters are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to the developer.

iCloud synchronization

If iCloud is enabled, ClassLoop uses Apple’s iCloud service to synchronize study data between devices signed in to your Apple Account. The ClassLoop developer cannot access this data.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit processes subscriptions and in-app purchases. The developer does not receive your full payment details.

Data sharing and tracking

ClassLoop does not sell personal information, show third-party ads, use cross-app tracking, or share study data with advertising or analytics providers.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local data remains until you delete it in ClassLoop or remove the app. iCloud data can be managed through Apple Account and iCloud settings. The developer holds no server-side profile or copy to delete.

Children

ClassLoop does not knowingly collect children’s personal information on a developer-operated server.

Changes

This policy may be updated when ClassLoop’s features or data practices change. The effective date identifies the latest revision.
