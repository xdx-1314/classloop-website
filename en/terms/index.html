Subscriptions and lifetime purchase

ClassLoop Pro offers monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before purchase and may vary by App Store region.

Your Apple Account is charged when you confirm your purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. When an eligible offer ends, the subscription converts to the selected paid plan unless canceled.
