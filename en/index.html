The Ebbinghaus forgetting curve

Every recall
strengthens your memory.

Memories fade over time. ClassLoop helps you strengthen them through spaced review. Cards you struggle to recall return sooner, while familiar cards wait longer.

A review session

Try to recall the answer first.

  1. Make flashcards

    Create cards by hand or import CSV or TSV files.

  2. Recall when cards are due

    Try to answer before flipping the card. Start each day with the cards due for review.

  3. Rate your recall

    Choose one of four ratings based on how difficult the card was. ClassLoop adjusts the next review accordingly.
