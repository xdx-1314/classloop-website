Privacy Policy

ClassLoop requires no account, displays no ads, and does not track you across apps. The developer does not operate servers that receive your class schedules, assignments, grades, focus records, or attendance records.

Information ClassLoop stores

Classes, assignments, countdowns, grades, attendance, preferences, focus records, and basic usage counts stay on your device and are not uploaded to the developer.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled, ClassLoop uses Apple’s iCloud service to sync study data across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The ClassLoop developer cannot access this data.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit processes subscriptions and in-app purchases. The developer does not receive your full payment information.

Data sharing and tracking

ClassLoop does not sell personal information, display third-party ads, track you across apps, or share study data with advertising or analytics providers.

Data retention, deletion, and your choices

Local data stays on your device until you delete it in ClassLoop or uninstall the app. You can manage iCloud data through your Apple Account and iCloud settings. The developer has no personal profiles or copies of your data on its servers to delete.

Children’s privacy

ClassLoop does not knowingly collect children’s personal information on developer-operated servers.

Policy updates

This policy may change when ClassLoop features or data handling change. The effective date shows when the policy was last revised.
